Teri Tareef... ✨

Tera husn itna gehra hai ke lafzon mein simat nahi paata,
Phir bhi dil se yeh kuch shayariyan tujh tak pahuncha rahi hain...

Teri aankhon mein sitare chhupaye hain,
Ek nazar mein dil ko chura lete ho tum...

Teri muskurahat jaise subah ki pehli kiran,
Har chehre ko roshan kar deti hai yeh ada...

Husn tera aisa ke chand bhi sharma jaaye,
Tujhe dekh kar taare bhi jhuk jaate hain...

Teri adaon pe dil fida hai har pal,
Tu chalti hai toh zindagi khubsurat lagti hai...

Teri har ek nazar mein jaadu hai chhupa,
Dil ki dhadkan badha deti hai bas ek hi baar...

Yeh sab lafz sirf tere liye... 💕
Tu hai toh har pal pyaara lagta hai.
